Projects

LightGCN Reproduction on MovieLens

  • Reproduced LightGCN (He et al., SIGIR 2020) against a BPR-MF baseline on MovieLens-small, with leakage-free graph construction and identical loss and embedding size.
  • After tuning on a validation split, LightGCN-1L improved NDCG@20 by 6.6% over BPR-MF in all 3 train/test splits and converged much faster (Recall@20 0.196 vs 0.064 at epoch 5).
  • The paper’s “more layers help” trend did not reproduce (1 layer > 2 > 3), and my early +21% result mostly reflected convergence speed.
